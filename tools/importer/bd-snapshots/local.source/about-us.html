Great coffee, made with care

Since 2011, Frescopa has been roasting exceptional, ethically sourced coffee — one small batch at a time.

Our story

Frescopa began with a simple belief: that a truly great cup of coffee has the power to bring people together. What started as a single roaster in a small corner shop has grown into a community of growers, roasters, and coffee lovers united by a shared passion for quality and craft.

Today we work directly with farming cooperatives across three continents, paying fair prices and investing in the communities that make our coffee possible. Every bean we roast is a promise — to our partners, to our customers, and to the planet.

More than a cup

We measure success not just in the quality of our coffee, but in the strength of the relationships behind it. From seed to sip, we obsess over every detail so you can enjoy a cup that's rich, balanced, and unmistakably Frescopa.

Whether you're brewing your first pour-over or your thousandth espresso, we're here to make every morning a little brighter.

What we stand for

Ethically sourced

Every order is roasted in small batches and shipped within 48 hours, so it arrives at peak flavor.

Craft in every cup

Our roasters taste and refine every profile, chasing balance, sweetness, and clarity in each origin.

Sustainable by design
